PROJECT
https://www.abdulsamad.shop (brand: Stylzo, single owner). Next.js Unicode text generator with ~100 font-style pages, category hubs, platform guides, symbol/emoji/kaomoji libraries and browser-only tools (QR generator and reader, image compressor, screenshot to text, PDF editor, PDF accessibility checker). Goals: get pages indexed, rank for narrow real search intent (not the head term "fancy text generator"), be citable by AI answer engines, and be ready for a Google AdSense review.

WHAT THE COMPETITOR REVIEW FOUND
Leaders (fontgenerator.cc, thefancytext.com, Picsart tools, FontSpace, loremipsum.io, fsymbols, lingojam, yaytext, coolsymbol) win through: many specific landing pages (per platform, game, style), long useful home-page content with tables and FAQs, strong authority and usage signals, gaming-name clusters (Roblox, Free Fire, PUBG, Mobile Legends, Fortnite, CODM, TikTok, X, Facebook, YouTube, Telegram, Snapchat) and extra languages. Stylzo's real advantages: typed text never leaves the browser, honest per-style support labels, per-style pages with specific explanations, an unusual browser-only tool set. Stylzo's gaps: only 6 platform guides, no game pages, short home page, English only, no visible authority signals.

HARD RULES (every phase)
1. PROTECTED PAGES: URLs in indexed-urls.txt keep their title, description, H1, body, canonical and JSON-LD unchanged unless I approve a specific edit. Before and after each phase compare their built HTML to production and report differences.
2. NO SPAM OR SCALED THIN CONTENT: no keyword stuffing, hidden text, fake reviews, fake ratings, invented download or usage numbers, copied competitor text, doorway pages, alphabet pages ("A in different fonts"), or pages differing only by a swapped keyword. Every new page needs at least 400 words of unique, useful content and something the generator alone cannot give, or it is not created.
3. EVERY FACTUAL RULE NEEDS A SOURCE: character limits, name rules and platform behaviour live in one data file where each fact has a source URL and a checkedOn date. Render only facts that have both. Omit anything unverified and list it in SEO-REPORT.md under "needs owner verification". Never guess limits.
4. Structured data only for visible content; validate it; no AggregateRating or Review.
5. No TODO, lorem or [name] in rendered HTML; add a build test.
6. Typed text must never be sent to the server, logged or stored. Add tests.
7. Small reversible changes with tests.
