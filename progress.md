# Font page content progress

## Rules for every batch

1. List A pages are untouched. Do not change their slug, title, meta description, H1, body, canonical, redirects or structured data.
2. Only list-B `/fonts/[slug]` pages receive editable content. Category hubs, `/styles/gaming`, `/embed` and the tool pages stay untouched.
3. Visitor-facing copy must not expose internal data. Never write phrases such as "the style is marked good", "support level", "this page", "catalog" or "editable".
4. Each page needs a 120-200 word style description, a fresh "Where it works" paragraph, three examples generated by the page converter, 2-3 style-specific FAQs and 4-6 related style links plus its category hub and `/fonts`. Never hand-type an example output.
5. FAQ questions must be specific to the style. No question or answer may repeat across pages, and at least one question per page must be unique to that page. Do not reuse a generic "Is X a font I need to install?" question with the same answer. Screen-reader questions are included only where accessibility is genuinely affected, and the wording must differ each time.
6. No two pages may share a paragraph. No two pages may share more than 30% of their sentences. Main text similarity must stay at or below 40%. Emoji-frame, kaomoji, `symbol-*` and `zalgo-*` pages must stay below 30% similarity within their group.
7. Titles must stay under 60 characters including ` | Stylzo`; meta descriptions must stay under 160 characters; each page has one H1.
8. Work in batches of 10. After each batch, show the completed slugs and two full sample pages, then wait for approval.
